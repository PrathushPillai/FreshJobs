FreshJobsBot

The crawler behind FreshJobs, a job portal for international students in the US.

What it does

FreshJobsBot reads the public job listings that employers publish for applicants: the job boards of their own career sites and hiring systems (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever and others). It lets students see new openings soon after they are posted, with a link back to the employer's own page to apply.

  • It reads only public job listings and job descriptions. It never signs in, never applies, never submits forms, and never collects personal data.
  • It follows each site's robots.txt, including disallowed paths and crawl delays.
  • It keeps request rates low: a limited number of requests at a time per platform, and it backs off on 429 Too Many Requests and 503, honouring Retry-After.
  • It never tries to get around access controls, bot challenges or CAPTCHAs. If a site blocks it, it stops.

How to recognise it

User-Agent: FreshJobsBot/1.0 (+https://freshjobs-prathu.duckdns.org/bot.html)

It sends requests only from the IP addresses listed at https://freshjobs-prathu.duckdns.org/bot-ips.txt.

Opting out or contacting us

To stop FreshJobsBot reading your site, add a robots.txt rule for FreshJobsBot (or for all user agents). To reach the operator, use Contact & support on the FreshJobs sign-in page.
